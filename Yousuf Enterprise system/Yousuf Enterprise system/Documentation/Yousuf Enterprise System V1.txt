Yousuf Enterprise Proposal (V1)Project Proposal & System Functional SpecificationProject Name: Web-Based Inventory, Ledger & Commission Management SystemPrepared For: Yousuf EnterprisePrepared By: Team Lead Software EngineerDocument Purpose: Non-Technical Business Overview & Complete Field Review1. Executive SummaryThis proposal outlines a web-based Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) application designed for Yousuf Enterprise to digitize daily business operations—including Owned Stock, Consignment (Commission) Stock, Dual-Role Parties (Vendors/Buyers), Multi-Head Ledgers, GST Invoicing, and Automated Cloud Backups.Written in non-technical English, this document provides a complete breakdown of every module and data field. This structure allows you to easily review, verify, add, or alter any field before system development begins.2. Key Business BenefitsDual-Role Party Architecture: Manage entities that act as both vendors and buyers under a single profile, eliminating duplicate records.Segregated Inventory Tracking: Keep company-owned purchases completely isolated from third-party consignment stock.Automated Commission Calculations: When consignment stock is sold, the system automatically calculates your commission percentage and routes the net balance to the stock owner's account.Multi-Head Ledgers & Mutual Balance Settlement (Contra Netting): Automatically offset mutual balances when a single party buys from and sells to Yousuf Enterprise.Flexible Tax Invoicing: Switch between GST and Non-GST invoices with a single click.Automated Google Drive Backups: Protect business data with daily encrypted backups uploaded directly to cloud storage.3.
